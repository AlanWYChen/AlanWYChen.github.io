# Web Legal, Privacy, and Consent

Consider only what applies:
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Cookie Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- refund/cancellation policy
- accessibility statement
- data export/deletion
- marketing opt-out
- cookie preference management
- consent records

If non-essential analytics/advertising tracking is used, consider:
- Accept All
- Reject All
- Manage Preferences
- granular categories
- ability to change preferences later
- withholding optional trackers until required consent is obtained

Flag jurisdiction-specific questions for legal review.
