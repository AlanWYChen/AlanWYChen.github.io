# Background Jobs and Queues

For jobs/events consider:
- idempotency
- retry policy
- exponential backoff
- jitter
- max attempts
- dead-letter queue
- poison-message handling
- deduplication
- ordering requirements
- visibility timeout/lease behavior
- job timeout
- cancellation
- observability
- progress/state reporting
- safe reprocessing
- transactional outbox/inbox patterns where needed
